During the mess of 2020 I decided to really learn to code. I chose Rust, started this blog to keep myself honest, and never stopped. The early posts are me working through the fundamentals one concept at a time; the newer ones are what came after — axum, Tera, and [Taxus](/projects/), the static site generator building the page you're reading right now.

I write the tutorials I wished I'd found back then: plain language, working code, no pretending I knew what I was doing on the first pass.

Newest posts are in [the blog](/blog/). The [about page](/about/) has the full story, [projects](/projects/) has the code, and the music lives at [crusty-metallian.net](https://crusty-metallian.net/).
