During the mess that was late 2020 I decided to really learn to code. I bounced on languages, but eventually landed on Rust. I started this blog to keep myself honest, which...didn't *really* work. The posts are a mishmash of me working through the fundamentals and simultaneously biting off more than I can chew.

I *want* to write the tutorials I wished I'd found back in 2022: plain language, working code, no pretending I knew what I was doing on the first pass. What's here will always morph to meet that goal.

It's a work in progress...just like the author.

Newest posts are in [the blog](/blog/). The [about page](/about/) has the full story, [projects](/projects/) has the code, and the music lives at [crusty-metallian.net](https://crusty-metallian.net/). Eventually will come photography.
